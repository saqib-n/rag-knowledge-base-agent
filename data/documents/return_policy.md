# Return and Refund Policy

## Return Window

Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, as long as they're unused, unworn, and in their original packaging with tags attached. Final sale items, marked clearly at checkout, cannot be returned.

## How to Start a Return

Contact support with the order number and the reason for the return. We'll email a prepaid return shipping label within one business day. Customers are not charged for return shipping on defective or incorrect items, a $5.99 return shipping fee applies to returns for any other reason, deducted from the refund.

## Refund Timing

Once we receive the returned item, refunds are processed within 3 business days. It can take an additional 5 to 10 business days for the refund to appear on the original payment method, depending on the bank or card issuer.

## Exchanges

We don't process direct exchanges. Return the original item for a refund and place a new order for the item you want instead, this is faster than waiting on an exchange to process.

## Damaged or Wrong Items

If an item arrives damaged or the wrong item was sent, contact support within 7 days of delivery with a photo. These cases skip the standard return process, we send a replacement or refund immediately without requiring the item to be sent back first, in most cases.
