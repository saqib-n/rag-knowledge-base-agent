# Shipping Policy

## Domestic Shipping

Standard shipping takes 5 to 7 business days and costs $4.99, or is free on orders over $50. Express shipping takes 2 to 3 business days and costs $14.99.

Orders placed before 2pm on a business day ship the same day. Orders placed after 2pm, or on a weekend, ship the next business day.

## International Shipping

We currently ship to Canada, the United Kingdom, and the European Union. International orders take 10 to 15 business days and cost a flat $19.99, regardless of order size. Customers are responsible for any customs or import duties charged by their country.

## Tracking

Every order gets a tracking number by email once it ships. Tracking updates can take up to 24 hours to appear after the order leaves the warehouse.

## Delayed or Lost Packages

If a package hasn't moved in tracking for more than 5 business days, contact support with the order number and we'll investigate with the carrier. If a package is confirmed lost, we reship at no charge or issue a full refund, customer's choice.
