# Remote Work Policy (Internal)

## Eligibility

Employees who have completed their 90 day probation period and whose role doesn't require regular in-person presence (retail, warehouse, and on-site support roles are excluded) may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.

## Core Hours

Remote employees are expected to be reachable on chat and available for meetings between 10am and 3pm in their local time zone, regardless of which days they're working remotely. Outside of core hours, flexible scheduling is fine as long as deadlines are met.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop and a one-time $300 stipend for a monitor, keyboard, and chair for employees approved for regular remote work. Equipment requests go through IT, not through the manager directly.

## Requesting Remote Days

Submit remote work requests through the HR portal at least 2 business days in advance for occasional remote days. Employees with a standing remote schedule (e.g. every Tuesday and Thursday) only need to set this up once with their manager, not request it weekly.

## Performance Reviews

Remote work arrangements are reviewed alongside regular performance reviews. There's no separate remote work review process, working remotely doesn't count for or against a performance rating on its own, only actual output and availability do.
